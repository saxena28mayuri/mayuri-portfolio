Something I built

for the go/no-go call

ReadAIness: release decisions you can defend

It holds a release, migration cutover or peak event against the gates a team agreed in advance, shows the evidence behind every result, and records who made the go/no-go call and why. It recommends. A named person decides.

Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Convex and Clerk

Try the live demo
  • Numbers are checked in code. No model decides whether one bug is more than zero.
  • No quote, no pass. The AI must quote the document word for word, and the quote is verified.
  • Unknown is never green. A gate with no evidence never counts as a pass.
  • Every override has a reason. Going against the recommendation needs it in writing.

How I work

the questions I keep asking

  1. Who owns this?

    Everyone owns a piece

    I work with everyone and make team members feel accountable, not just informed.

  2. What does done mean?

    One bar for all owners

    I align owners on one bar, so every team is working toward the same finish line.

  3. Where is that written?

    Status and decisions stay in writing

    Nobody should need to remember who agreed to what.

  4. What could go wrong?

    Risk gets raised early

    I say it while there’s still time to do something about it.

  5. What are we trading off?

    Trade-offs stay in the open

    I negotiate scope and priorities with senior business, product and engineering leaders to protect the outcome.
